The Cup of Life

The Cup of Life game was the product of the final assessment in my first year of my Advanced Diploma of Professional Game Development at AIE. It was a group project with 9 of us from the Art, Design and Programming disciplines. I was in charge of programming the coffee machine, all the ingredients and the UI elements as well as helping the other team members with issues they faced. This game was in development from September to November 2020.
